Process

Data in.
Intelligence out.

Three steps. No data team required. The Brain handles the rest.

01 — Connect
Link your data sources
Point Business Brain at your databases, documents, and tools. It ingests them in real time and begins building the knowledge graph immediately.
02 — Build
The Brain maps everything
AI extracts entities, relationships, and patterns across your data. Scheduled analysis runs automatically — weekly summaries, anomaly detection, trend reports — without being asked.
03 — Ask
Query in plain language
Ask anything about your company. Every answer is retrieved from your actual data — not a language model's general knowledge. If the answer isn't in your data, the Brain says so.
