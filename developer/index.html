Platform

One platform.
Every department.

Six departments. One data source. No more copy-pasting between spreadsheets or losing leads in email chains.

01 — Sales & CRM
Full lead lifecycle
From first call to signed contract. AI captures every inbound lead, routes to sales, and tracks the pipeline — unit by unit, project by project.
02 — Financial
Live P&L per project
Budget vs. actual tracked in real time. Payment milestones, vendor invoices, and revenue all flow into one financial view per project.
03 — Permits
Never miss a deadline
Track every filing, approval, and inspection. Automated reminders before every deadline so nothing slips through the cracks.
04 — AI Call Intake
24/7 lead capture
An AI agent answers every call — even after hours. Lead details captured and routed to Sales within seconds. No lead goes cold while the office is closed.
05 — Architecture
Docs, plans, revisions
Architectural drawings, engineering reports, and revision history — organized per project, searchable by every department that needs them.
06 — CEO Dashboard
See everything at once
One live view: all projects, all departments, all numbers. Know exactly where every project stands without calling anyone.
