The Business Brain

One AI layer that knows
your entire business.

Most companies have data everywhere and intelligence nowhere. Decisions are made from memory, knowledge walks out when people leave, and the same questions get answered from scratch every week. The Business Brain is the system that changes that — it maps your knowledge, schedules its own analysis, and makes every AI interaction smarter over time.

01 — Core Data
Everything in one place
Documents, contracts, emails, decisions, workflows — all ingested, structured, and made queryable. The Brain reads what your team produces.
02 — Scheduled Analysis
Patterns, automatically
Recurring analysis jobs surface what your team would never have time to find: trends, anomalies, obligations coming due, risks building quietly.
03 — Embeddings
Meaning, not just keywords
Every document and decision is converted to a vector embedding. The Brain understands context — the difference between a standard clause and a negotiated exception.
04 — RAG Layer
AI that knows your company
Agents query the Brain in real time. Every answer, every automation, every alert is grounded in your actual data — not generic LLM training.
