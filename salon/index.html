01 — Daily Entry
Revenue in seconds
Log services, retail, and repairs in one tap — split by cash, card, and QuickPay. No more entering the same number in three different sheets.
02 — Stylist Payroll
Every stylist, every week
Per-stylist daily amounts tracked, weekly summaries auto-generated. Staff changes don't break anything — add or remove stylists in seconds.
03 — Smart Finance
Tax rules, just handled
Cash tax (8.875%) and credit card tax (4.5%) calculated automatically. The 40% bank allocation is enforced as a rule — not a formula that breaks.
04 — Owner Dashboard
Your salon at a glance
Real-time P&L, cash vs. card split, bank deposit totals, and monthly comparisons — all visible from a phone. No calling the front desk.
05 — Bookkeeper Tools
Reconciliation, done
End-of-day cash vs. credit reconciliation, deposit confirmations, and monthly P&L reports — all generated automatically, no spreadsheet assembly required.
06 — AI Insights
Know what's working
Which services are most profitable, which days drive the most revenue, which stylists are growing. Patterns you'd never spot in a spreadsheet, surfaced automatically.
