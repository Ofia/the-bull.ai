Case & matter management, built in.

The Bull isn't only a contract tool — it's a lightweight matter manager for solos and small teams. Every matter tracks its own clients, opposing parties, and key dates, with tasks and deadlines for filings, hearings, and deliverables attached directly to it. Client records and documents live on the matter they belong to, searchable across your whole practice. And when a contract tied to that matter — an engagement letter, a fee agreement, a settlement — creates an obligation, The Bull turns it into a task linked back to the matter automatically. One system, not two.

Privacy

Your clients' data
stays yours.

Legal data is the most sensitive data there is. We built The Bull Legal with a layered privacy architecture — so your contracts and your clients' information stay exactly where they belong. For lawyers, every matter and contract is isolated by firm and user, with a full audit trail for compliance. For asset managers, your lease and vendor data stays scoped to your organization, with complete logs of who saw what and when.

Retrieval Isolation
We never send full contracts to the AI. Only the excerpts relevant to each query are retrieved — the rest of your document stays in our encrypted database, inaccessible to the AI layer.
Firm-Scoped Workspaces
Every query is hard-filtered by your firm's ID at the database level. No data from another firm can appear in your workspace — isolated by architecture, not policy.
Full Audit Trail
Every AI query, document upload, and automated action is logged with a timestamp and user identity. Full compliance evidence available on demand.
Zero Data Retention Coming soon
Our AI provider (Anthropic — SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 & ISO 42001 certified) retains API data only briefly for security and reliability before automatic deletion. We are working toward a zero-data-retention configuration as we scale.
Building toward SOC 2 Type II certification. All current controls are operational and enforceable by contract today. Enterprise clients receive a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) on request.
Web3 · Decentralized Party Network

Every party gets
their own window in.

Each contract gets a private peer network built on Web3 infrastructure. The lawyer spins it up in seconds, shares a link, and every party joins with their own cryptographic identity — seeing only what's theirs, communicating through a signed thread.

One Network Per Contract
The lawyer creates a dedicated peer network for each agreement. Contract data is published instantly — no server, no database, no intermediary. The network lives on the peers connected to it.
Invite by Link
A unique access link and shared credentials let each party in. They authenticate with a cryptographic keypair derived from their password — the same concept as a Web3 wallet, with no account created on any server. They see only their obligations.
Signed Contract Thread
All parties communicate through a live peer feed — confirmations, document uploads, and updates. Every message is cryptographically signed, timestamped, and replicated across the network.
